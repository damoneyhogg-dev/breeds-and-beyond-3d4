# Breed & Beyond — Design Improvements

These improvements borrow useful design patterns from open-world tracking and simulation games without copying their art, code, weapons, or animal-hunting gameplay.

## Priority systems

1. **Readable onboarding**
   - A short Coco tutorial teaches movement, scent evidence, safe searching, cooldowns, and entering an event.
   - Every new mechanic gets a plain-language explanation and can be replayed.

2. **Living search world**
   - Weather, time of day, terrain, water, wind, and distractions change scent difficulty.
   - Tracks show age and confidence instead of giving away the answer immediately.
   - Search locations rotate so missions do not feel repetitive.

3. **Meaningful dog behavior**
   - Each dog has focus, confidence, stamina, curiosity, scent strength, and recovery needs.
   - Breed is a starting tendency only; training and individual personality matter more.
   - Coco's calm focus and scent talent are her signature strengths.

4. **Better progression**
   - Earn ribbons, virtual coins, titles, equipment, safe travel routes, and new regions.
   - Improvement, sportsmanship, and safe completion earn rewards—not only first place.
   - Puppies, seniors, and recovering dogs receive adapted events.

5. **Mission variety**
   - Hidden clothing-article trials
   - Treasure searches
   - Lost-pet marker searches
   - Room and vehicle searches
   - Trail tracking and relay searches
   - Weather and distraction challenge events

6. **Player-friendly presentation**
   - Clear map icons, event requirements, expected difficulty, and reward previews.
   - Pause, save, resume, adjustable text size, color-safe status indicators, and mobile-friendly controls.
   - Optional hints that cost no real money and never punish new players.

7. **Care and safety feedback**
   - Visible hydration, fatigue, heat, stress, and recovery indicators.
   - Referee or vet stoppage when a dog needs rest.
   - Positive reinforcement only; no aggression training or dog-versus-dog contact.

## Build order

1. Competition hub and fair matching — prototype complete.
2. Map destinations and Coco's hidden-article trial — in progress.
3. Search evidence, weather, terrain, and distraction system.
4. Dog-care dashboard, daycare, vet, and nutrition guide.
5. Larger regions, hound leagues, transport, titles, and optional co-op search.
